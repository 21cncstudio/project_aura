# Project Aura v1.2.0 beta.2

Beta update for the Aura AQ 4.3-inch and 7-inch controllers.

## Changes since v1.2.0 beta.1

- Shows the localized `WARMUP` label on the main HCHO card while the active
  SFA30 or SFA40 driver reports that its measurement is warming up.
- Hides the HCHO numeric value and `ppb` unit during warmup, matching the VOC
  and NOx card behavior and preventing a stale measurement from being shown.
- Restores the HCHO value and unit after warmup and preserves the existing AQI
  fallback when no HCHO measurement is available.

## Hardware and compatibility

The environments remain `project_aura` and `project_aura_7`; the targets remain
`aura-aq-v1` and `aura-aq-7-v1`. The 7-inch profile remains `7_dual_i2c` and
uses its separate target-specific package.

SFA40 warmup remains status-driven. SFA30 keeps its existing power-up warmup
policy and does not claim a new warmup period after an ordinary software restart.

## Validation boundary

Native tests cover AQI fallback, valid HCHO measurement display, warmup-only
display and suppression of a stale valid value during warmup. Both production
firmware environments build successfully. The exact release binaries still
require a visual cold-boot and restart check on their matching boards.
