# Project Aura v1.1.6 beta

This beta introduces separate, explicitly identified firmware profiles for the
Aura AQ 4.3-inch and 7-inch controllers. It is intended for controlled
validation and is not a 7-inch public-release approval.

## Highlights

- Keeps the existing 4.3-inch panel and external sensors on their established
  shared I2C topology.
- Adds a dedicated 7-inch profile with panel devices on I2C0 and external
  sensors plus GP8403 on I2C1 at SDA GPIO44 and SCL GPIO6.
- Keeps GPIO43 disconnected from the 7-inch sensor clock path and leaves ESP32
  internal pull-ups disabled for the externally pulled-up sensor bus.
- Uses native USB CDC on the 7-inch profile and reports the hardware target,
  hardware profile, build ID, and source-bound prerelease version at startup.
- Carries forward only independently reviewed post-1.1.5 fixes; experimental
  COM7 recovery behavior remains disabled.
- Separates panel and sensor readiness, ownership, shutdown, OTA validation,
  and Last Known Good eligibility on the 7-inch topology.
- Uses an address-only, non-mutating GP8403 ACK probe without undocumented
  register reads or payload writes.
- Keeps the normally disabled CH422G diagnostic profile-safe: `0xFF` remains
  the 4.3-inch image, while the 7-inch profile uses `0xD1` to keep native
  Type_C2 selected.
- Keeps unqualified raw panel-line samples from latching an I2C fault, and
  keeps expected optional-sensor absence out of user alerts without hiding
  real faults or removing diagnostic logs.
- Adds a device-side hardware-target check to built-in web OTA. Opposite-model
  and unlabelled legacy BINs are rejected before flash access, with a persistent
  inline warning and no OTA restart. Protection applies only after installing
  a firmware containing the guard; see [migration and limits](../OTA_HARDWARE_TARGET_GUARD.md).

## Candidate and evidence status

- The following build/package evidence is the historical `fac6e30` snapshot.
  The later `0b68e92`, `35001ed`, and built-in OTA guard changes need their own
  exact-candidate build and physical-test evidence; do not reuse the older
  artifact identity for them.
- That cleanup snapshot contained 25 implementation/documentation commits after
  `origin/main` and before its final documentation update.
- Exact `fac6e30` 4.3-inch and 7-inch binaries were built, test-key packages
  were accepted by the final Aura Link verifier, and a cross-target check was
  rejected as intended.
- Coordinated local control-plane revisions are Aura Link `e826b46` and Aura AQ
  site `85cb5f8`; they are not production deployment evidence. Aura Link plus
  migration `0044` currently provide fail-closed identity storage/policy, not a
  trusted 7-inch enrollment mechanism and not an unlock.
- Neither exact firmware artifact has completed physical release
  qualification, and no branch or package was pushed, deployed, or published.

## Release boundary

- The 4.3-inch and 7-inch artifacts are not interchangeable.
- The public 7-inch installer and Aura Link OTA lanes remain locked until the
  selected exact candidate passes the documented 100 kHz physical cold-boot baseline and the
  trusted one-time identity-enrollment path is implemented and reviewed.
- Aura Link migrations and the installer-site revision require one coordinated
  non-rolling maintenance cutover. Firmware mutations must be stopped, legacy
  unversioned attempts drained or rejected, migrations `0042` through `0044`
  applied in order, inventory/backfill and strict database checks completed,
  and Aura Link plus the site switched together. An application-only rollback
  to the old attempt formula or missing-identity inference is not supported.
- A later 400 kHz panel-bus experiment, if needed, will be a separate 7-inch
  test and will not change this baseline.
